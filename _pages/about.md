## About Me

I am an Artificial Intelligence student at Kunming University of Science and Technology, with an undergraduate background in Automation. My interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and AI-generated content.

I have hands-on experience with deep learning model deployment, AI image generation, and data processing. I have independently deployed and experimented with Stable Diffusion WebUI and other AI models, and have experience running deep learning models both locally and in cloud computing environments.

I am also a digital creator on Pixiv, where I have built a community of more than 20,000 followers through AI-assisted creative work. This experience has allowed me to explore the practical applications of generative AI while developing a strong interest in the intersection of technology and digital creativity.

### Research Interests

- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- Generative AI and AI-generated Content
- Deep Learning Model Training & Deployment
- Data Processing and Pattern Recognition
- AI Applications in Scientific Research

### Selected Experience

I have participated in research involving mass spectrometry data processing, feature extraction, one-hot encoding, similarity analysis, and pattern recognition using Python.

I have also developed and contributed to open-source tools related to RVC technology and experimented with the deployment of AI applications using cloud computing platforms.

My long-term goal is to explore the application of artificial intelligence to real-world problems while combining technical research with creative applications of generative AI.
